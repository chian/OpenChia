# Divide by decisions and preserve evidence

RefineParts owns a scope's decomposition, next-problem selection and contribution
to the whole. Parts are behavioral problems whose results can be assessed, not
automatic replicas of target Episode nodes or separate departments for activities.

## Choose meaningful nesting

Use a child when it can refine a scoped problem internally and return enough
admitted information for the parent's next decision. Mandatory preparation,
implementation and acceptance stay ordered inside the owner of that work.
Do not expose them as optional peers that a selector could run out of order.

Only RefineParts may issue DesignPart work or a proper nested Parts scope. A
Designer can call its authorized coder and support children, but new independent
design work returns to the Parts owner. This also prevents a specialist from
escaping a cross-part conflict by delegating a narrower private version of it.

## Specify both information products

Decide what a child must retain for its own next unit: exact candidates, previous
approaches, local measurements, dependencies, lessons and unresolved questions.
Then define the smaller parent projection needed to choose, accept or coordinate.

Parent-facing content should include exact request/assignment correlation,
candidate and measure identities, supported determinations, changed dependencies,
relevant failures, unknowns, and the decision needed. Preserve original evidence
references for drill-down. The host projects admitted records; a model-written
summary cannot create a verdict or turn repeated assertions into corroboration.

Siblings receive necessary dependency deltas through their common owner, not
unfiltered transcripts. They can share campaign facts without sharing one giant
model context. A missing relevant conflict cannot be hidden by a short display;
authoritative eligibility must still inspect the full applicable state.

## Integrate on one candidate

Each admitted change invalidates affected checks immediately. A pass on revision
R and another pass on revision S do not prove their joint condition. Reuse evidence
only when the declared dependency rules establish applicability to the candidate.

Report changes and opposing regressions during the loop, not just after a child
succeeds. Exact revisits and repeated opposing regressions warrant a host-grounded
conflict report. Similar labels alone do not prove that new evidence has no value.

The nearest Parts owner covering both sides can issue a joint assignment, repair
a bad measure/dependency/boundary, justify reopening, or expose an irreconcilable
specification gap. Keep earlier history and close replaced assignments explicitly.

The parent's credit measures its own demonstrated contribution, not child score
totals. Restoring old passes under fresh child IDs is not new progress. Final
acceptance names one exact candidate with applicable evidence for all mandatory
conditions, or an honest unresolved frontier.
