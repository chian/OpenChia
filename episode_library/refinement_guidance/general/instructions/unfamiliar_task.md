# General route for an unfamiliar task

Not fitting an existing specialty is a reason to reason from the actual contract,
not a reason to reject the build or manufacture a new runtime class.

## Establish what the target must mean

Identify its inputs and admissible domain, repeated unit, outputs, observable
effects, dependencies and enclosing use. Separate claims about the artifact from
claims about the world. A simulator can implement a model correctly while the
model is a poor account of reality; a report can be schema-valid while unsupported.

List required behavior in terms someone other than the implementer can examine.
For each requirement, record the existing evidence, an observation that would
show failure, and what would justify acceptance. Preserve unknowns explicitly.

Find the nearest useful *property*, not just the nearest topic name:

- Deterministic quantities suggest exact reference calculations and invariants.
- Search or reasoning suggests separate candidate validity and answer adequacy.
- Stochastic behavior needs model assumptions and a declared statistical measure.
- Nested behavior needs scoped handoffs and same-candidate composition checks.

These are selection hints for focused guidance. They do not authorize applying
all specialties or importing their sample acceptance criteria into the task.

## Choose a bounded first approach

Propose the smallest change that can distinguish an actual design hypothesis.
Identify its local measure and protected conditions before handing it to the
implementer. Make missing prerequisites explicit. If a trusted instrument exists,
reuse it only within its documented domain.

If no mechanical oracle exists, name the exact evidence or authorized human
judgment required, including its limits. Do not substitute another model's
agreement, a self-assigned score or an endless hierarchy of judges.

## Report enough to let the owner decide

Return the supported interpretation, inspected source identities, proposed or
admitted approach, measured result, failed alternatives and remaining uncertainty.
A catalog miss is a guidance gap, not proof that the target is impossible. A
measurement gap is not a pass. A task-meaning change goes through the appropriate
approval path rather than being smuggled in as an implementation detail.

The reusable Designer still owns implementation and part acceptance. New design
subproblems return to RefineParts. Generality comes from applying this process to
unfamiliar requirements, not from unrestricted delegation or mutable criteria.
